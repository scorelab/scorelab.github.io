**Sustainable Computing Research (SCoRe)** lab is a long-standing research group in University of Colombo School of Computing (UCSC), Sri Lanka. SCoRe lab spearheaded frontier computing research and contributed various innovation in real-world applications.
Originated as Wireless Ad-hoc Sensor Networks (WASN) lab in UCSC in 10th February 2006, which was later renamed as SCoRe lab, the group has came a long way with over a two decades of experience in conducting funded research with real-world impact.
